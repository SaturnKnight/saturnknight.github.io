Privacy Policy — MediaBot

Last updated: 23 November 2026

MediaBot is a personal, non-commercial tool used by its owner only. It records a history of media files the owner downloads and stores that history in the owner's own Google Drive account.

MediaBot requests only the drive.file scope, which grants access solely to files and folders MediaBot itself creates. It cannot read, modify, or view any other file in the user's Google Drive.

No data is collected from third parties, sold, or shared with anyone. All data stays within the owner's own Google account.
